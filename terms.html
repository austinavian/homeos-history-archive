Terms of use

Effective October 10, 2026.

HomeOS History Archive is a personal tool maintained by austinavian for authorized Home Assistant history collection, Google Drive storage, and authenticated historical queries. This documentation website does not offer access to a public archive service.

Authorized use

Use the application only with accounts, systems, and data you own or are authorized to access. You control Google authorization, archive configuration, access credentials, and any decision to share query results with another service.

Storage and historical accuracy

Storage is bounded, and the oldest verified archive-owned records may expire at the configured cap. Collection outages, source exclusions, and missing historical exports can leave gaps. Queries should be interpreted together with their source provenance and coverage reports. Timestamp formatting does not establish a physical sensor's timing accuracy.

Keep independent backups when needed. The tool is under active development and provides no promise of uninterrupted collection, indefinite retention, or complete recovery. It should not be relied on for safety-critical decisions.

Privacy and third-party services

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. Google Drive, GitHub Pages, Home Assistant, and any connected query provider have their own applicable terms and policies. This project is not affiliated with Google, GitHub, or Home Assistant.

Stopping use and contact

You can stop the worker and revoke its Google authorization at any time. Existing archive and local copies require separate deletion. Questions can be directed to the maintainer through the linked GitHub profile; do not post private history or credentials publicly.

These terms describe this personal application, not a commercial hosted service.
